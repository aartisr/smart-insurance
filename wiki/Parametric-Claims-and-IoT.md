## 1. Parametric Weather Oracles
Traditional catastrophe insurance involves months of adjuster visits and receipts. Parametric coverage replaces subjective assessments with objective mathematical triggers:
- **Hurricane Peril**: Binds to the NOAA National Hurricane Center anemometer network. If sustained wind speed exceeds 110 mph within a 5-mile radius, a pre-agreed payout of $10,000 is wired automatically.
- **Earthquake Peril**: Binds to the USGS Global Seismographic Network. Payouts trigger immediately when peak ground acceleration (PGA) crosses 0.35g.
- **Freeze & Pipe Burst**: Binds to municipal temperature stations and in-home smart water meters.

## 2. Connected Device Deflation (IoT Telematics)
Policyholders connect their smart devices for continuous risk assessment and instant rate deflation:
- **Flo by Moen / Phyn Water Shutoff**: 15% immediate rate discount upon telemetry confirmation of active automatic shutoff capability.
- **Smartcar Connected Vehicles**: Reads mileage directly from the vehicle CAN bus. Safe drivers with low night-time driving receive up to 38% monthly premium refunds.
