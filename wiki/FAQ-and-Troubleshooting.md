### Q1: What is Aequitas?
**Answer**: Aequitas is an AI-native property & casualty (P&C) insurance operating system that eliminates traditional 35%–42% broker markups. It combines Google Gemini vision underwriting, live IoT telematics risk deflation, instant parametric claim settlement, and sub-3-second commission payouts via FedNow.

### Q2: How does Aequitas settle broker commissions in under 3 seconds?
**Answer**: Unlike legacy carriers that hold commissions for 30–60 days, Aequitas runs an atomic 80/18/2 revenue split upon premium collection. Available commissions are instantly disbursed directly to bank accounts via the Federal Reserve's FedNow network or Stripe Connect Instant Payouts.

### Q3: How do parametric insurance claims work without adjusters?
**Answer**: Parametric insurance relies on cryptographic weather and sensor oracles (such as NOAA hurricane wind speed stations or USGS earthquake monitors). When a verified trigger threshold is met, payments are pushed automatically to the policyholder's bank account in seconds without requiring damage receipts or adjuster visits.

### Q4: Which insurance carriers and lines are supported?
**Answer**: Aequitas supports Auto, Home, Renters, Commercial, and Cyber lines through certified connectors including Boost Insurance MGA, Sure Embedded Platform, and the EZLynx 40+ carrier rating hub.
