Module 3

Sub-3-Second Instant Commission Payouts (FedNow & Stripe Connect)

Traditional insurance brokerages hold agent commissions for 30 to 60 days. Aequitas utilizes an atomic 80/18/2 revenue split triggered upon customer payment capture:

  • 80% Carrier Risk Pool: Transferred directly to the underwriter or fronting reinsurance pool.
  • 18% Agency Commission: Immediately credited to the broker ledger for instant cashout.
  • 2% Community Surplus Fund: Redistributed annually to low-claim policyholders.

Brokers can initiate instant withdrawals via FedNow / RTP (<1.5s, $0 fee) or Stripe Connect Instant Payouts (<3.0s) directly to commercial bank accounts.

Module 5

Frequently Asked Questions (AEO & Search Engines)

Q: Is Aequitas compatible with licensed insurance carriers in all 50 US states?

Yes. Through embedded Program Administrator partnerships (Boost Insurance, Sure) and comparative rating APIs (EZLynx), Aequitas interfaces with A-rated paper admitted in all 50 states.

Q: How do users receive instant parametric claim payouts?

When authorized oracles (e.g. NOAA weather sensors or Flo by Moen automatic valves) confirm a threshold event, payouts are wired immediately to the user's connected checking account or debit card in seconds without adjuster paperwork.
